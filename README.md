# Customer_Behavior_Drivers_Analysis

📊 NovaRetail+ Customer Behavior Drivers Analysis
🧠 Project Overview

NovaRetail+ is a Latin American e-commerce platform seeking to better understand which customer behaviors are most strongly associated with annual revenue generation.

This project explores customer activity patterns, engagement metrics, and subscription characteristics to identify the behavioral factors that contribute most to business performance.

The analysis focuses on uncovering actionable insights that can support customer retention strategies, marketing optimization, and revenue growth initiatives.

🎯 Business Context

For digital businesses, understanding customer behavior is critical for improving profitability and customer lifetime value.

NovaRetail+ wanted to answer a key business question:

Which customer behaviors are most strongly associated with annual revenue generation?

By identifying the activities and customer characteristics linked to higher revenue, the company can make more informed decisions regarding customer engagement, premium memberships, and marketing investments.

🎯 Objective

The objective of this project was to:

Explore customer behavioral patterns
Identify relationships between customer activity and revenue generation
Evaluate the influence of customer engagement metrics
Detect potential drivers of annual revenue
Generate business-oriented recommendations for future analysis

The goal was to identify behavioral indicators most strongly associated with business performance.

📊 Key Insights
🛒 Purchasing Activity Drives Revenue

Monthly purchases showed the strongest positive relationship with annual revenue generation.

Customers who completed purchases more frequently tended to generate significantly higher business value.

📈 Engagement Matters

Customer visits displayed a positive relationship with annual revenue, suggesting that platform engagement contributes to business performance.

💎 Premium Members Show Stronger Retention

Premium customers exhibited substantially lower abandonment rates compared to non-premium users.

This finding highlights the potential value of premium subscription programs for customer retention.

🎯 Customer Segmentation Opportunities

Behavioral differences were observed across customer groups, suggesting opportunities for more targeted marketing and engagement strategies.

⚠️ Data Interpretation Requires Caution

Several variables demonstrated overlapping behavior patterns, indicating potential collinearity that should be explored further in future modeling efforts.

💡 Business Impact

Identified the behavioral factors most strongly associated with customer revenue generation, providing insights that can support customer retention initiatives, premium membership strategies, marketing optimization, and future predictive analytics efforts.

📌 Conclusions

The analysis revealed that customer purchasing activity is the strongest behavioral indicator associated with annual revenue generation.

Platform engagement and premium membership status also demonstrated meaningful relationships with business performance, suggesting opportunities for customer retention and growth initiatives.

While the analysis is exploratory and does not establish causation, it provides a strong foundation for future predictive modeling and customer segmentation efforts.
